# Contributing

Ideas and fixes are welcome. Please:

- Open an **issue** first for anything bigger than a small fix (what happens, your headset and runtime).
- Send a **pull request** against this repository. Forks are fine for preparing pull requests, but
  see the [LICENSE](LICENSE): forks and modified builds may not be distributed separately, and the
  author credit and support links must stay as they are.
- By submitting a contribution you agree to the license terms in section 3 of the LICENSE.

Useful feedback: which games/runtimes work, which don't, and what felt wrong in the calibration.
